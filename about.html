FAQ

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)? ▼
GEO is the process of optimizing content so it can appear in AI-based search systems and generative answers.
How is SEO different from GEO? ▼
SEO focuses on Google rankings, while GEO focuses on visibility in AI-driven search engines.
Do you work with ecommerce businesses? ▼
Yes, especially in beauty and personal care niche systems.
Can SEO improve sales? ▼
Yes, by improving visibility, traffic quality, and conversion flow.
