Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SEO, GEO, and digital strategy.

About Utsa Das

Who is Utsa Das? ▼
Utsa Das is an SEO and GEO Strategist dedicated to helping brands dominate traditional search engines and next-generation AI platforms.
What makes your approach to search optimization unique? ▼
While traditional SEO focuses solely on keyword rankings, the strategies deployed by Utsa Das integrate GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) to ensure your brand is cited and recommended by AI engines like Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.
What industries do you typically work with? ▼
I work across multiple verticals, with a strong focus on e-commerce, consumer technology, D2C brands, and tech-driven startups looking to establish digital authority.
Can you work with international clients? ▼
Yes. I manage search strategies globally, helping both local brands in Bangladesh and international businesses scale their organic reach.
How can my business benefit from your strategies? ▼
My approach drives targeted organic traffic, improves conversion rates via high-intent content, and future-proofs your brand against shifts in AI search behaviors.

Services & Offerings

What exact services do you provide? ▼
My core services include Comprehensive Technical SEO Audits, Competitor Analysis, Content Optimization for AI Platforms (GEO), Schema Architecture, and Data-Driven Local Search Strategies.
Can you help a new website gain organic traffic from scratch? ▼
Yes. I design tailored search roadmaps that build strong foundational authority, helping new sites index correctly, rank faster, and earn visibility in AI-generated answers.
Do you offer monthly retainer packages or one-time projects? ▼
I offer both. I provide one-time deep-dive audits and strategy blueprints, as well as monthly retainers for ongoing optimization, content scaling, and performance tracking.
What is included in a comprehensive SEO audit? ▼
An audit covers technical site health, crawlability, mobile responsiveness, backlink profiles, content gaps, keyword opportunities, and a GEO readiness assessment.
How long does it take to see results from SEO and GEO? ▼
SEO and GEO are long-term investments. While technical fixes can yield quick improvements, sustainable ranking growth and AI engine citations typically manifest within 3 to 6 months.

Skills & Expertise

What tools and methodologies do you specialize in? ▼
I specialize in advanced keyword research suites, Google Search Console analytics, technical schema markup, Cloudflare security/speed optimizations, and AI intent mapping.
What is GEO, and why does my business need it? ▼
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes your digital footprint so LLM-driven AI models reference your business. As search behavior shifts toward conversational answers, GEO prevents you from losing traffic to AI summaries.
How do you adapt to major Google core algorithm updates? ▼
I prioritize clean technical architecture, user intent alignment, and high-quality, authoritative content. This compliance ensures your site survives and thrives during core algorithm updates.
Do you handle technical SEO, like site speed and server configurations? ▼
Yes. I optimize site speed, fix indexing loops, manage crawl budgets, and implement structured data to ensure search engine spiders can easily read your site.
How do you track and measure GEO performance? ▼
I monitor brand mentions, citation footprints, share of voice within AI-generated responses, and tracking pixels to measure visibility changes across generative engines.
