About Utsa Das

Utsa Das is an SEO and GEO Strategist dedicated to helping brands dominate traditional search engines and next-generation AI platforms. With a background in computer technology, he bridges the gap between technical execution and strategic business growth.

While traditional SEO focuses solely on keyword rankings, the strategies deployed by Utsa Das integrate GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). This ensures your brand is not just ranking, but being cited and recommended by AI engines like Gemini, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.
