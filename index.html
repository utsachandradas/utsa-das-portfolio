Frequently Asked Questions

Utsa Das is a founder, SEO strategist, and GEO optimization specialist based in Bangladesh. He helps brands build technical search visibility, scale e-commerce platforms, and optimize content for both traditional search engines and AI systems.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the process of structuring website content and entities so that AI-driven search engines, like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, accurately cite and recommend your brand in their generated answers.

SEO focuses on ranking web pages in traditional search engine results through links, keywords, and technical structure. GEO focuses on being the primary cited source in AI-generated responses through entity recognition, semantic depth, and authoritative context.

Users are rapidly shifting from traditional search queries to conversational AI prompts. Optimizing for AI search ensures your brand remains visible, authoritative, and recommended in the exact platforms where your future customers are asking questions.
